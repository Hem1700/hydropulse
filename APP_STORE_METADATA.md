## 5. Full Description

```text
Enter deep flow without burning out or dehydrating. 

When you are deeply focused, hours disappear and dehydration sets in, causing brain fog, eye strain, and fatigue. HydroPulse unites Pomodoro interval focus with mindful hydration — rewarding every completed focus sprint with a refreshing sip break.

WHY HYDROPULSE?
• DUAL ACTIVITY DIAL: An outer radial ring monitors your focus countdown, while an inner liquid wave visualizes your daily water intake.
• MINDFUL SIP PROMPTS: When your 25-minute focus session ends, a resonant singing bowl chime and gentle haptics remind you to drink water and rest your eyes before the next sprint.
• IMMERSIVE AUDIO & SPOTIFY: Focus to seamless offline soundscapes (Gentle Rain, Forest Stream, Deep Brown Noise, Lo-Fi Pulse) or launch curated Spotify focus playlists. HydroPulse's audio engine is tuned to mix smoothly over your music without interrupting.
• 1-TAP QUICK HYDRATION: Log a glass (200ml), mug (350ml), or bottle (500ml) in a single tap with water-drop haptics.
• STREAKS & INSIGHTS: Track your daily focus minutes, hydration milestones, and build unbroken habit streaks.
• 100% PRIVATE & ON-DEVICE: No accounts, no logins, no cloud tracking. All your habits stay strictly on your device.

Key Features:
- Customizable Focus, Short Break, and Long Break durations
- Adjustable daily hydration targets (1,000ml to 4,000ml)
- High-fidelity offline audio loops & Spotify deep-linking
- Rich physical CoreHaptics & Tibetan singing bowl chimes
- Sleek Oceanic Dark and Clean Light themes
- Local background notifications for completed intervals

Elevate your productivity and wellness today with HydroPulse.
```

## 7. App Review Notes (For Apple Reviewers)
When submitting for review, paste this into the "Notes" field for the Apple review team:
```text
HydroPulse is an offline-first productivity and wellness companion combining a Pomodoro focus timer with mindful hydration tracking.

- No user credentials or registration are required to use the app.
- Audio playback includes built-in offline ambient soundscapes as well as optional deep-linking into the Spotify app for curated focus music. Audio sessions are configured with mixWithOthers so external audio is never interrupted.
- All focus intervals, water intake logs, and streak counters are stored locally on the device using standard local persistence.
- Local notifications alert users when a focus session or sip break completes.
```
