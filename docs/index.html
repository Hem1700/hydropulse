💧 HydroPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Welcome to HydroPulse ("the App"). Your privacy is our highest priority. HydroPulse is intentionally engineered as an offline-first, zero-tracking application.

1. Data Collection & Usage

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data.

  • All focus timer durations, completed intervals, water intake logs, and streak counters are stored locally on your device using on-device encrypted storage.
  • We do not require you to create an account, register an email, or sign in.
  • We do not utilize any third-party tracking, analytics, or behavioral advertising SDKs.

2. Background Audio & Spotify

HydroPulse provides built-in offline ambient soundscapes and links to open Spotify playlists. When you open Spotify, you interact directly with the Spotify application under Spotify's Privacy Policy. HydroPulse never collects or monitors your Spotify credentials, listening history, or account details.

3. Local Notifications

HydroPulse may request permission to send local notifications to alert you when a focus session or hydration break has completed. These notifications are generated solely on your local device and do not involve remote push notification servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
